# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want SwarmSpindle discussions and contributions to be welcoming, respectful,
and constructive. Participate in good faith, assume good intent, and focus
feedback on the work rather than the person.

## Expected behavior

- Use inclusive, professional language.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Respect privacy and permission boundaries.
- Keep reports, logs, and examples free of credentials and personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized
attention without consent, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.
Publishing private information or secrets is not acceptable, even in a bug
report or example.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a concern privately to [tnt850910@aol.com](mailto:tnt850910@aol.com).
Include enough context to investigate, but do not send credentials, private
configuration, or unredacted run exports. Maintainers may remove harmful
content, limit participation, or take other action appropriate to the report.
